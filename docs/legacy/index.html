FAQ

Questions, answered

What makes IMG different from other agencies?
We're a small Inland Empire studio, not a volume shop. Your work is handled personally by the people you hire, your calls get answered, and every site and campaign is built custom for your business and this market.
What is AI Search Visibility?
More and more customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for recommendations instead of Googling. AI Search Visibility is the work of structuring your website, listings, and content so those AI tools know who you are and recommend you for local searches.
How does Local SEO work?
We optimize your Google Business Profile, website content, citations, and reviews so you rank in the Map Pack and on Page 1 for the searches your customers actually make — "plumber in Corona," "tacos near Ontario Mills," and so on.
How long does a website take?
Most sites launch in 2–3 weeks. Larger builds with e-commerce or lots of pages can take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Who actually does the work?
We do. Strategy, design, copywriting, and campaign management are all handled in-house. Nothing is white-labeled or shipped to a subcontractor you've never met.
Do you require long-term contracts?
No. Website projects are flat-scope, and marketing services are month-to-month. We keep clients with results, not paperwork.
What areas do you serve?
The Inland Empire first — Riverside and San Bernardino counties, including Riverside, San Bernardino, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, Fontana, Corona, Moreno Valley, Redlands, Temecula, and surrounding cities. We also work with businesses beyond the IE remotely.
What does the free audit include?
We review your website, Google Business Profile, local rankings, and AI search presence, then walk you through what's working, what's costing you customers, and what we'd fix first — no obligation.
Can you help if my business is outside the Inland Empire?
Yes. Our local-first playbook works anywhere — we just happen to know the IE best. If you're elsewhere in SoCal or beyond, reach out and we'll talk it through.
