# Southern California city targeting

Research date: September 15, 2026. This is a qualitative launch hypothesis, not a keyword-volume forecast or a claim about which city will be most profitable. No Google Ads account, Keyword Planner export, CPC data or qualified-lead history was available.

## Recommended sequence

Start with a small number of markets so the budget can produce useful evidence. Riverside is the anchor. Test Corona and an Ontario/Rancho Cucamonga cluster as the first nearby expansion. Keep Anaheim and Pomona as distinct tests because the offer, audience and county context differ. The Inland Empire page is a regional destination; do not let a broad IE campaign overlap all the city tests without exclusions.

## Campaign design hypothesis

Use separate commercial themes such as custom website design, business website redesign and web design agency. Begin with controlled phrase/exact coverage and review actual search terms. Seed negatives may include jobs, salary, course, tutorial and template searches; inspect actual intent before blanket exclusions. Do not exclude brand-builder terms automatically if redesigning those sites is a valid service.

For a local-presence experiment, select people in or regularly in the target area. Google defaults to presence or interest; location matching is a best-effort signal, not exact geofencing. City names in copy do not set campaign geography. [Google location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en)

Keep overlapping city and regional targets mutually intentional through campaign geography/exclusions. Separate Anaheim if its budget and offer differ. Only split Ontario/Rancho into independent budget tests when volume supports it. No budget amounts are recommended without the owner's budget and minimum project economics.

## Decision criteria

Record delivered inquiries, qualified inquiries, meetings and won work by city. Compare cost per qualified inquiry and eventual project value, not raw form rate alone. Reject spam and out-of-scope inquiries from the optimization signal. Use real lead volume to choose the review period; do not declare significance after an arbitrary number of days. Next data needed: Keyword Planner local forecasts, historic search terms if any, budget, service coverage, close rate and project value.
